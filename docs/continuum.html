Arabic Continuum: the ladder ran backwards.

Do nearby words in Classical Arabic share roots and patterns more than chance allows? Yes, in every layer tested. The surprise is the order. Once each layer meets its own baseline, the finest layer leads and the coarsest comes last.

01 // Cohesion lift1,642 roots. 11 verb forms. 49,968 tokens.Verified Ladder retracted

Count, shuffle, compare.

A pattern means little until the shuffled text has had its turn.

A token is one counted word piece. A layer is a way of grouping those pieces: the surface form (raw), the consonant skeleton without vowels (rasm), the light stem, or the triliteral root. Cohesion asks how often neighbours inside a window of W words collide in the same group.

The first report compared raw cohesion across layers. Roots won. That win was built in. Fewer, larger groups collide more often even in scrambled text. The repair gives each layer its own null: shuffle the order 250 times, recount, and divide. That quotient is the lift.

All 12 cells (4 layers at windows 10, 20 and 40) beat their nulls. Each cell scores p = 1/251. After Holm-Bonferroni correction for 12 tests, p_adj = 12/251 = 0.0478. Holm-Bonferroni is a stricter threshold that pays for testing many things at once.

02 // Ring and inclusio79 surahs with 20 or more verses.Mirror falsified

A mirror that was not there.

A ring would pair verse i with verse L-1-i, so the second half mirrors the first. The test compared true mirror pairs with shifted and shuffled pairs across 79 long surahs. The mirror scored 0.040319. The pooled null scored 0.042126. The mirror sits below chance, with lower-tail p = 5/251 = 0.0199. The pointwise mirror is falsified.

A softer idea followed: the opening tenth and closing tenth might share more than either shares with the middle. That pattern is called inclusio, a frame that opens and closes a text. It was formed after the mirror failed, on the same 79 surahs, so it starts life as exploratory.

Six Quran slices were tested, with and without the ten most frequent words removed. All six fail Holm-Bonferroni at B = 250. The best adjusted value is 42/251 = 0.1673, well above 0.05. Framed chapters in Kalila score higher (+0.009739) than the Quran set (+0.008283). The honest label is EXPLORATORY_INCONCLUSIVE_NOT_SEPARATED. The idea is neither confirmed nor separated from ordinary framed prose.

Odd and even surah halves appear in the code as split-half internal consistency checks. They are the same corpus seen twice. They are never called unseen or held-out data.

03 // Translation loss9 named roots. 2 English versions.Descriptive

What English lets go.

Pick verses linked in Arabic by a shared root. Then ask how often the English keeps any shared word at all. On nine hand-picked roots (ftq, rtq, sbH, mdd, wsE, flk, kwr, qdr, zwj), 50.91% of linked pairs share no English content word in Saheeh International. In Pickthall the figure is 65.88%.

Unrelated verse pairs score 89.70% and 92.30% on the same measure, so the root link does survive in part. The root list was chosen by the authors for its physical and structural flavour. It was not sampled at random. Read the two percentages as a named, descriptive measurement. The lexicon study gives the same question a full null and distance controls.
