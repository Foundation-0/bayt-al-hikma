01 // Polymath lineageAUDIT-092R2. 13 scholars. 14 cited relations.Verified offline

Baghdad to Maragheh.

Every theorem rebuilt from scratch. Every name tied to a printed edition.

The core table runs from al-Khwarizmi (c. 780 to 850) to Nasir al-Din al-Tusi (1201 to 1274). Baghdad dominates the early centuries. Then the centre of gravity moves: Basra and Cairo, Bukhara and Isfahan, Cordoba, Diyarbakir, Maragheh.

Each scholar carries at least one construction the code verifies exactly. Al-Khwarizmi: his six canonical equation forms, including x squared plus 10x equals 39, root 3. Thabit ibn Qurra: amicable pairs (220, 284) and (17296, 18416), and a parabola area trapped between 13233/20000 and 13433/20000 around 2/3. Ibn al-Haytham: the mirror reflection quartic, with no rational root, so no ruler-and-compass solution. Al-Tusi: the Tusi couple, two circles whose combined motion runs in a straight line with zero sideways residual.

Two falsafa names anchor the philosophy on this site. Ibn Sina (980 to 1037) argued with al-Biruni (973 to 1048) over whether matter is continuous or atomic. The code checks their geometric obstruction: on a square lattice, diagonal and side atom counts would need M squared to equal 2N squared, which no integers satisfy. Ibn Rushd (1126 to 1198) attacked epicycle models that fit anything. The code keeps a small witness of that complaint: a model with too many freedoms fits its training points exactly and misses a held-back point by 45.

Tributaries in, branches out.

The second lineage study widens the map. Four tributary scholars feed the core: the Hellenistic and Indian sources the Baghdad translators read. Fifteen form the core. Four carry the work onward to Maragha, Damascus, Samarkand and Europe. The span is c. 287 BCE to 1543 CE.

From India, Brahmagupta: a cyclic quadrilateral with area K = 1764 and circumradius R = 65/2, the bhavana composition for D = 92, and the full 13-step chakravala method that solves x squared minus 61y squared equals 1 in integers. From the Greek world, Archimedes: the 96-gon bracket 223/71 < pi < 22/7, checked with strictly conservative rational bounds. Apollonius contributes the focal reflection property. Heron contributes the degenerate limit and a square root routine.

Downstream, two comparisons draw care. Ibn al-Shatir and Copernicus use matching double-epicycle geometry for the Moon and Mercury. The code verifies the numerical equivalence (L1 distance 1/9000 in the stated encoding). How the model travelled, if it travelled, stays an open historical question. The site claims the arithmetic match and nothing more. Al-Ghazali and Ibn Rushd disagree over causation. The code keeps a 2 by 2 witness where observation alone cannot separate their two stories, while an intervention can (average effect 9/25 against 0).

At Samarkand, al-Kashi computes sin of 1 degree by fixed-point iteration with contraction factor L = 1/625, matching 9 of 9 leading base-60 places. His pi bracket from a 3 times 2 to the 28 polygon confirms 16 decimal digits and contains his 1424 CE value of 2 pi. In music, al-Farabi ratios such as 12/11 and 27/22 sit at least 49 and 45 cents from any equal-tempered semitone. A cent is one hundredth of a semitone. The neutral intervals of that tradition cannot live on a modern piano grid.

04 // What the graph check provesAnd what it does not.

Consistency, not discovery.

Both lineage studies encode scholars as nodes and relations (taught, corresponded with, extended, critiqued) as directed edges in active voice: source did the verb to target. The checker then refuses the table unless every node and edge carries a structured citation (work, edition, location), every lifespan is sane, no one acts before age 15 or after death, tiers run tributary to core to branch, and the influence order has no cycles.

The certificate says encoding_consistency_only = True. Passing proves the encoded table obeys its own stated rules and cites its sources. It does not prove the history is complete or the relations are the only reading. That boundary is the point. A fail-closed checker keeps sloppy tables out. Judgement about the past still belongs to historians.

Parallels to modern ideas (feedback control in al-Jazari valve, fixed points in al-Kashi, overfitting in Ibn Rushd complaint) are labelled conceptual analogies in the reports. No machine-verified bridge to any modern repository is claimed.
